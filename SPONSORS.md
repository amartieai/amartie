# Sponsoring AMARTIE

> Why AI companies should sponsor the framework that proves their models aren't swapped.

---

## The Problem for AI Labs

Your models are good. But users don't trust them. Why?

Because aggregators swap models silently. When a user pays for your model and gets a cheaper one, **your reputation suffers.** You didn't do anything wrong — but you get the blame.

---

## The Solution

AMARTIE generates receipts that prove which model served every response. If your model was the one that answered, **the receipt says so.** If it wasn't, the receipt says that too.

**Receipts build trust. Trust drives adoption.**
